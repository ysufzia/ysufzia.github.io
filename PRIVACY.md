# Port of Kings — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 25 August 2026*

Port of Kings is a single-player strategy game. It has no accounts, no logins, no analytics, and no
server of its own. This policy describes everything the app does with data, and it is short because
the app does very little.

---

## Your game data stays on your device

Saved chronicles, achievements, statistics, your language choice and your settings are stored in the
app's own private storage on your phone. **They are never transmitted anywhere.** Uninstalling the
app deletes them.

If your device has Android's automatic backup enabled, Android may copy that storage to *your own*
Google account so your game can be restored if you reinstall. That is a function of your device and
your Google account, not of this app — we never see it, and we cannot access it.

## We collect nothing ourselves

The game makes no network requests of its own. There is no analytics package, no crash reporting,
no telemetry, no profile, and no account. We do not know who you are, that you installed the game,
or that you played it.

## Advertising

This app shows optional advertisements supplied by **Google AdMob**.

Ads are never forced on you: they appear as a card you can decline, and watching one is always your
choice in exchange for something in the game.

To serve those ads, AdMob may collect your device's **advertising identifier** and information
about your device, and may use them to show personalised advertising. You can reset or delete that
identifier at any time in **Settings → Privacy → Ads** on your Android device, and you can opt out
of personalised advertising there.

What Google does with that data is described in Google's own policy:
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads>

## Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed **entirely by Google Play**. We never see, receive or store
your payment details. What we receive from Google is confirmation that a purchase succeeded, which
the app records on your device so your purchase is honoured.

## Children

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from
children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app changes what it does with data, this page is updated before that version is released.
The date at the top is the date of the most recent change.
